About Us

Indian Institute of Management and Manufacturing (IIMM), Ayodhya

Indian Institute of Management and Manufacturing (IIMM), Ayodhya is a newly emerging institution located in the culturally significant city of Ayodhya, Uttar Pradesh. Established with the goal of bridging traditional academic learning and modern industrial demands, IIMM aims to deliver quality education in the fields of management, entrepreneurship, and manufacturing technologies. Life Of IIMM

The Indian Institute of Materials Management (IIMM) has approved a new branch in Ayodhya, the city of Lord Ram, to support the "Ek Bharat Shresth Bharat" initiative. The inauguration will be an illustrious event featuring prominent figures from the healthcare industry, academic institutions, hotel and hospitality sectors, and other industries, along with IIMM dignitaries. This initiative aims to enhance the nation's growth and development. The event promises to be memorable and impactful, highlighting the importance of collaboration across various sectors. Learners, researchers, scholars, and professionals interested in contributing to nation-building are invited to engage with this new branch. For more information and to get involved, individuals can contact the Branch Executive Members or visit the IIMM website at [www.iimm.org](http://www.iimm.org). Target Group: Working Professionals in the field of Stores, Purchase, SCM, Finance, Accounts, Logistics, Materials Management etc and students pursuing their UG/PG degree or diploma.
